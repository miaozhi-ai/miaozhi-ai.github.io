Children's Healthy Use Policy

Last updated: September 2026

CHENGDU MIAOZHI TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD. (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to promoting safe, responsible, and healthy use of our applications. This Children's Healthy Use Policy describes the measures and principles we follow to help reduce excessive use and encourage appropriate digital habits.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to our applications and services where children's use or access may be relevant. It is intended to supplement our Privacy Policy and does not replace the parental controls, age requirements, or other safety features provided by Apple, Google, or other applicable platforms.

Different countries and regions may have different legal requirements concerning children and minors. Where applicable, we follow the requirements of the jurisdictions in which our applications are distributed.

2. Promoting Healthy and Balanced Use

We encourage users to maintain a reasonable balance between digital activities and everyday life. Our applications are not designed to encourage users to remain engaged for unnecessarily long periods of time.

Where appropriate, we encourage users to take breaks and to balance screen-based activities with sleep, physical activity, outdoor activities, education, family time, and social interaction.

3. Reducing Excessive or Prolonged Use

Depending on the application and platform, certain products may provide reminders or other mechanisms intended to encourage users to take periodic breaks.

The availability and behavior of such features may vary by application, operating system, device, region, and version. We do not represent that every application provides a specific usage-time management or break-reminder feature.

4. Parental and Platform Controls

Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to use the parental controls and screen-time management tools provided by the relevant operating system or app store.

  • Apple devices may provide Screen Time, parental controls, age restrictions, and purchase controls.
  • Android devices and Google services may provide family management, parental controls, and screen-time tools.
  • Parents or guardians should review the age rating and available settings before allowing a child to use an application.

These platform-level controls can help parents or guardians manage access, purchases, usage time, and other aspects of children's device use.

5. Age Requirements and Children's Privacy

Unless an individual application expressly states otherwise, our applications are not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Where local law establishes a higher minimum age for the collection or processing of children's personal information, the applicable legal age will apply.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information that is unnecessary for the operation of the application.

Our handling of personal information is described in our Privacy Policy.

If you believe a child has provided personal information

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate parental or guardian involvement, please contact us at miaozhi.ai@gmail.com . We will review the request and take appropriate action where required by applicable law.

6. Responsible Product Design

We aim to design our applications in a way that does not intentionally encourage compulsive or excessive use.

Where applicable, we seek to avoid product mechanisms that deliberately pressure children to continue using an application solely to maintain engagement.

Features, content, advertising, and purchase options may vary between applications and regions and are subject to the applicable platform requirements.

7. Purchases and Advertising

Where an application contains advertising or in-app purchases, the availability and presentation of those features may depend on the application, platform, region, and applicable laws.

Parents and guardians should use the purchase restrictions and parental controls provided by their device or app store to prevent unauthorized purchases.

We do not encourage children to make purchases or provide payment information without appropriate authorization.

8. Encouraging Offline Activities

We encourage children and families to maintain a healthy balance between digital products and offline activities, including:

  • Regular physical activity and outdoor play
  • Healthy sleep and rest
  • Education and learning
  • Family activities and communication
  • Face-to-face social interaction

9. Safety, Complaints, and Feedback

Parents, guardians, users, and other concerned individuals may contact us if they have concerns about children's safety, excessive use, inappropriate content, privacy, advertising, or other issues related to our applications.

We will review relevant reports and, where appropriate, take reasonable measures consistent with applicable law and platform requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to our applications, services, legal requirements, or platform policies.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date shown at the top of this page. Continued use of our applications after an updated policy becomes effective means that the updated policy will apply to the extent permitted by law.

Privacy Policy

儿童健康使用政策

最后更新：2026年9月

成都妙至科技有限公司（以下简称“我们”） 重视儿童及未成年人的安全、隐私和健康使用体验。 本《儿童健康使用政策》说明我们在减少过度使用、 倡导合理数字使用习惯以及保护儿童相关权益方面所遵循的原则。

1. 政策适用范围

本政策适用于涉及儿童使用或访问情形的相关应用及服务。 本政策作为 《隐私政策》 的补充，不取代 Apple、Google 或其他适用平台提供的 家长控制、年龄限制及其他安全功能。

不同国家和地区对于儿童及未成年人可能有不同的法律要求。 在适用的情况下，我们将遵守应用发布和提供所在地区的相关法律法规。

2. 倡导健康、合理的使用方式

我们倡导用户保持合理的数字使用习惯，在使用应用与现实生活之间 保持适当平衡。我们的应用不会以鼓励用户进行不必要的长时间使用 为产品设计目标。

在适当情况下，我们鼓励用户合理安排休息，并平衡数字活动与 睡眠、体育锻炼、户外活动、学习、家庭活动及社会交往。

3. 减少过度或长时间使用

根据具体应用及平台情况，部分产品可能提供使用提醒、 休息提示或其他旨在帮助用户合理安排使用时间的功能。

相关功能是否提供以及具体表现可能因应用、操作系统、 设备、地区和应用版本而有所不同。 我们不表示所有应用均提供特定的使用时长管理或休息提醒功能。

4. 家长及平台控制功能

我们建议家长或法定监护人合理使用相关操作系统和应用商店 提供的家长控制及屏幕使用时间管理功能。

  • Apple 设备通常提供“屏幕使用时间”、家长控制、 年龄限制及购买限制等功能。
  • Android 设备及 Google 服务可能提供家庭管理、 家长控制及屏幕使用时间管理功能。
  • 家长或监护人在允许儿童使用应用前，应关注应用的年龄分级 以及设备和应用商店提供的相关设置。

这些平台级控制功能可以帮助家长或监护人管理儿童的应用访问、 使用时间、购买行为及其他设备使用情况。

5. 年龄要求与儿童隐私

除非具体应用另有明确说明，我们的应用不以在知情情况下 直接收集13岁以下儿童个人信息为目的。

如果适用法律规定了更高的儿童个人信息收集或处理年龄要求， 则以相关法律规定的年龄要求为准。

我们不会在非必要情况下主动要求儿童提供个人信息。

有关个人信息的收集、使用、存储和保护方式， 请参阅我们的 《隐私政策》。

如果您认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息

如果您认为儿童在未经适当的家长或监护人参与的情况下 向我们提供了个人信息，请通过 miaozhi.ai@gmail.com 联系我们。 我们会根据具体情况进行核实，并在适用法律要求的范围内 采取适当措施。

6. 负责任的产品设计

我们致力于以负责任的方式设计和运营应用， 不以故意诱导儿童进行强迫性或过度使用为产品目标。

在适用情况下，我们会避免通过不合理的设计机制， 故意向儿童施加持续使用应用的压力。

不同应用中的功能、内容、广告及购买选项可能有所不同， 并会受到具体应用、地区以及适用平台规则的影响。

7. 购买与广告

如果具体应用包含广告或应用内购买，其提供方式和展示形式 可能因应用、平台、地区及适用法律法规而有所不同。

我们建议家长或监护人使用设备和应用商店提供的购买限制及 家长控制功能，以防止未经授权的购买行为。

我们不会鼓励儿童在未经适当授权的情况下进行购买或提供 支付信息。

8. 鼓励参与线下活动

我们鼓励儿童及家庭在数字产品使用与现实生活之间保持健康平衡， 合理安排以下活动：

  • 体育锻炼和户外活动
  • 充足的睡眠与休息
  • 学习和教育活动
  • 家庭活动与交流
  • 面对面的社会交往

9. 安全问题、投诉与反馈

家长、监护人、用户以及其他关注儿童安全的人员， 如果对儿童安全、过度使用、不适当内容、隐私、广告 或我们的应用存在其他疑问，可以联系我们。

对于收到的相关反馈和报告，我们会进行合理审查， 并在适用法律法规及平台规则要求的范围内采取适当措施。

10. 政策更新

我们可能根据应用功能、服务内容、法律法规或平台政策的变化， 不时更新本政策。

更新后，我们会修改本页面顶部的“最后更新”日期。 在政策更新后继续使用相关应用的，表示更新后的政策将在法律 允许的范围内适用。
