Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CHENGDU MIAOZHI TECHNOLOGY CO., LTD. ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our mobile applications and related services.

This policy applies to applications distributed through Apple App Store, Google Play Store, and other authorized distribution channels, where applicable.

1. Information We Collect

The information processed by an application may vary depending on the features you use, your device settings, and the services integrated into that application.

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, application version, language, and certain technical or performance information.
  • Usage Information: Information about how features are used, interactions with the application, diagnostic information, and crash reports.
  • Location Information: Certain applications may use device location information when you enable location-based features, such as maps, navigation, or GPS tracking. Location access is controlled through your device permissions.
  • Approximate Location: Certain third-party services may derive approximate location, such as country or general region, from network information such as IP address.
  • Advertising and Analytics Information: Advertising identifiers, app-related identifiers, diagnostic information, and similar technologies may be processed by third-party advertising or analytics services.

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information solely to use the basic functionality of applications that do not include account-based services.

The specific information processed by each application may differ according to its functionality and the third-party services it uses.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information for the following purposes, depending on the features and services provided by the application:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve application functionality.
  • Process requests and provide features requested by users.
  • Monitor application performance and reliability.
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical problems.
  • Understand general usage trends and improve the user experience.
  • Display and measure advertising where advertising is included and permitted by applicable law.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security issues, or other activities that may harm our services.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations.

3. Maps & Location Services

Some of our applications provide map display, location-based features, GPS tracking, route recording, location markers, distance or area measurements, or related functionality.

When you grant location permission, the application may access location information to provide the requested functionality. Depending on the feature, certain location data may be processed locally on your device.

The use of a map or location-based feature does not by itself mean that your precise location is transmitted to our servers. However, third-party map, advertising, analytics, or other service providers integrated into an application may process information as necessary to provide their services.

You can control location access through your device's privacy and location settings. Disabling location access may affect features that require location information.

Important Notice

When using map-related functions, users should comply with applicable laws and regulations, including laws and regulations concerning surveying and mapping, national security, and the protection of state secrets.

Activities involving map data may include, but are not limited to, placing markers, drawing shapes, measuring distances or areas, recording routes, importing or exporting geographic data, and sharing location-related information.

Users should avoid collecting, processing, storing, sharing, or transmitting information relating to military restricted areas, military administrative areas, locations of national security authorities, or other sensitive areas involving state secrets.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use, storage, sharing, export, or transmission of map and location data complies with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Health and Device Data

Some applications may provide health, fitness, wearable-device, or sensor-related features.

Where an application requests access to health or fitness data, such access is controlled through the relevant operating system permissions and is used only to provide the corresponding application functionality.

The availability and processing of health or fitness information may vary between applications. Please review the permissions presented by the application before granting access.

No method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining application functionality, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and complying with legal obligations.

Information stored or processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their respective policies and applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your device, location, and applicable law, you may have certain choices or rights regarding information processed through our applications.

  • Manage application permissions through your device settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or other control over certain personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information provided below. We may need to verify your request before taking action.

  • 监测应用运行情况和稳定性。

用户应主动避免收集、处理、保存、分享或传递涉及军事禁区、 军事管理区、国家安全要害部门所在地，以及其他涉及国家秘密 的敏感区域的数据。
